# Sound credits

Every sound in this folder comes from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) and is
released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain). Thank you to all the recordists!

Each clip was cut from the original recording's high-quality preview, trimmed to
its start, peak-normalised, faded and re-encoded as mono mp3 by
`tools/build_sounds.py`. Footsteps were picked out of longer recordings, and the
ambience loops were crossfaded so they repeat smoothly. The three kid screams were
prepared earlier for the game Swoopin.
